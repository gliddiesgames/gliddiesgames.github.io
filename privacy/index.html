Privacy Policy.

We believe magical adventures should come with clear, straightforward information about privacy. This policy explains how the Gliddies game handles information.

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 22, 2026

1. Who we are

Gliddies is a game developed and published by Gliddiesgames. For privacy questions, contact gliddiesgames@gmail.com.

2. Accounts and information you provide

The current planned test release of Gliddies does not require an account or registration. Players are not asked to provide their name, email address or other personal details to play.

3. Game progress

Gliddies is designed to store gameplay progress and preferences locally on your device. Local game data is used to remember your progress and settings. Removing the app or clearing its data may erase this information.

4. Advertising, purchases and analytics

The initial test version is intended to be released without in-game advertising, in-app purchases or third-party analytics. These features may be introduced in a future release. If they are introduced, this policy and the relevant Google Play disclosures will be updated before release to explain the applicable data processing.

5. Platform and technical data

When you download or use Gliddies through Google Play or an Android device, Google and your device provider may independently process technical information under their own privacy policies. This policy does not cover data collected independently by those services.

6. Children’s privacy

Gliddies is intended to be suitable for a broad audience, including children. We do not intentionally ask children to submit personal information within the game. If you believe personal information has been provided to us, contact us at the address below.

7. Sharing and retention

We do not intend to operate a server-side player account database for the initial test version. Locally saved game progress remains on the device until the player clears app data or uninstalls the game, subject to device backup settings. If you contact us by email, we use your message and contact details to respond and retain correspondence only as reasonably needed for support or legal obligations.

8. Your choices

You can remove locally stored game information using your device’s app-data controls or by uninstalling the game. You may contact us to ask about information you have sent to us directly by email.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Gliddies evolves. The latest version will be published on this page, with its updated date. Material changes to data handling will be reflected in this policy and in applicable app-store disclosures.
